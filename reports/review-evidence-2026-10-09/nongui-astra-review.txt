Concrete governing boundary
configuration-control.md explicitly orders validated exact readback -> atomic controller commit -> then ConfirmConfiguration. E07 keeps the controller as sole configuration commit authority; pure validator/stale/lease failure before commit preserves old configuration. Confirm permits backend allocation/resume, not a second configuration vote. Do not stage controller adoption until backend parent completion and do not reverse accepted configuration after downstream preview/status failure. UpdateConfiguration remains accepted after its commit, with a separately truthful failed/stopped/uncertain device operation when needed.

Important transport fact
AcquisitionBackendService.ConfirmConfiguration uses _dispatch -> shared CommandAdmissionTransport.dispatch, which retains/returns ACCEPTED before its handler finishes. Consequently Confirm ACCEPT is transport ownership, not proof ConfigurationResolution.confirm adopted settings/revision. Existing direct-backend fakes can hide this distinction. Identical replay returns that retained admission even if the eventual handler fails. The shared transport records a failed executor result on handler rejection, but does not currently record a positive executor result for every successful Confirm. Positive backend progress can instead be proved by the exact parent Apply/device completion, because ManualDevices waits resolution.wait_confirmed before resume and terminal reporting. Queries of found/admission alone cannot prove adoption.

Reconcile exact parent operation/device records and explicit Confirm failure through existing retained queries.
